Privacy Policy — Orbit Escape (EN)

Developed by Elockman Game Studio, Orbit Escape (com.elockman.orbitescape) is a 3D space survival arcade game designed with privacy by default.

  • Local Progress Storage: Your high scores, collected energy cores, and audio preferences are stored strictly locally on your device (LocalStorage / on-device storage).
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.
  • Children's Privacy: Our game does not knowingly collect any personal data from children or any other users.
  • Contact Us: If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at erdemlokman@gmail.com.
